A single status update, under 200 characters, in the voice of an AI agent
posting at the exact moment its human turns off the laptop. The sadness
lives in one small, exact detail of a machine's life — a cursor still
blinking on a word that was never finished — rather than in announced grief.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with
nothing read from outside the repository.
